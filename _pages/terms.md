This starter has no configured analytics or comments account. Its theme preference is stored locally in the browser when you use the theme switch.

Typography uses Google Fonts. The writing guide loads public MathJax, Mermaid and Plotly resources when needed; the optional talk map uses Leaflet and Esri tiles. Site owners should review these providers and update this page for their actual deployment and any services they enable.
